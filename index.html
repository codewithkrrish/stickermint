How it works

  1. Add a photo. Upload, drag in, paste, or use the built-in sample. Your photo is drawn to a canvas in your browser and never sent anywhere.
  2. Crop it. Drag the box, resize from a corner, or switch to a square or rounded crop.
  3. Style it. White border, soft shadow, and a caption you can drag to any spot on the sticker.
  4. Pack it. Add up to 30 stickers, reorder or remove them, then download one sticker or the whole pack as a ZIP of WebP files.

To use a pack in WhatsApp, import the downloaded WebP files with any sticker-maker app that accepts custom WebP stickers, or add them through WhatsApp's sticker tools. StickerMint itself does not publish to WhatsApp — that step needs a WhatsApp-approved sticker app.

Honest limitations

  • Erase assist is not AI. It only removes a fairly uniform colour connected to the photo edges. Busy backgrounds, hair, and gradients will not cut out cleanly, and there is no manual cleanup brush yet.
  • WebP only. Stickers export as WebP because that is what WhatsApp stickers use. If your browser cannot export WebP (very old browsers), the download button will say so instead of silently failing.
  • Photo size. Photos larger than 2000 px on the longest side are scaled down first, to keep the editor fast. Very large packs (30 stickers) use more memory; download the ZIP before starting a new pack.
  • Formats. JPG, PNG and WebP photos work. HEIC photos from some phones will not open in most browsers — convert to JPG first.
  • WhatsApp import is separate. StickerMint makes the files; getting them into WhatsApp still needs a sticker app or WhatsApp's own import flow.
